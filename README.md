## customer_revenue_analysis

# Overview

This project explores customer purchasing behavior using transactional e-commerce data. The main objective is to analyze revenue distribution and identify patterns in seasonality and trends. The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation techniques, and time-series methods to better understand the structure of customer revenue and identify strategically important customer groups.

# Main Goals

- Analyze the distribution of customer revenue
- Identify repeat vs one-time customer behavior
- Evaluate revenue concentration using Pareto analysis
- Investigate revenue trends and seasonality over time

# Dataset

The project uses the Online Retail dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. The dataset contains transactional records from a UK-based online retailer, including:

- Invoice information
- Product descriptions
- Quantities purchased
- Unit prices
- Customer identifiers
- Transaction timestamps

The dataset spans approximately two years of customer activity.
